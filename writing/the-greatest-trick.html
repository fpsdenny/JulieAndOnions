This wasn't no movie
The smart thing was to say yes, get up and leave the room
But then I thought to myself, what if this was a movie?

Riley Freeman, The Boondocks, 'The Funraiser'

Once upon a time, on a tiny rock spinning around a fiery ball of gas, lost on a vast, dark and empty napkin. Some featherless bipeds started to cause some big trouble, by munching on some funky fungus. For millions of years, scrounging for food, running from predators, and fucking, had held humans in its warm embrace. In a single moment reality was shattered and some very unlucky monkeys were not only literally out in the cold, but they became the first to experience being figuratively out in the cold. The destiny of man in an instant is realized and comes crashing down to Earth, where it must be built up from the dust. So it was done, brick by brick over thousands of years the foundation for the future was laid. Until one day, the great fruit of the vine of life was ripe enough to be picked.

Imagine being invited to see the cockpit of an air-plane in flight, once you enter only to find it empty and the door locked behind you. You are now the proud pilot of your own aircraft, but this effect may be somewhat dampened by the realization that every second you spend learning to fly is a second the vessel is steering itself. It would be foolish, to expect a God which can give more than it must take. A puppet on a string follows the same laws as the puppet-master, and a thin veil hangs between a performance and reality. For all man will ever know his double edged sword was taken up at the beginning and the end of time, all its' meanings encompassed. Of all the knowledge and tools developed and refined, one thing stands above them all.
