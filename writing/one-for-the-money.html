God grant me the serenity to accept the things I cannot change; courage to change the things I can, and the wisdom to know the difference.

The Serenity Prayer

Why is it, that every day of our lives is a winding road? Forever and always, reaching towards a destination that is not destined to be reached. There are those who believe, and those who do not, why we are all here in the flesh. To watch a movie, fall in love, or work until you die the cards will fall one by one in the game of liar's poker.

Nothing that is worth doing can be achieved in our lifetime; therefore we must be saved by hope.

Nothing which is true or beautiful or good makes complete sense in any immediate context of history; therefore we must be saved by faith.

Nothing we do, however virtuous, can be accomplished alone; therefore we must be saved by love.

No virtuous act is quite as virtuous from the standpoint of our friend or foe as it is from our standpoint. Therefore we must be saved by the final form of love which is forgiveness

Reinhold Niebuhr, The Irony of American History

Who on this Earth is the strongest? The smartest? The most loving, or courageous? Some might say it is the one who will feel, some will say it is the one who will do, others say it is the one who dares. For some getting out of bed every morning takes all the strength Earth will offer them, others find the strength every day to offer away all that they have. Who am I to judge?

If you fly do it to death. It's only one God and it's only one crown so it's only one king that can stand on this mound.

Pusha T, Numbers on the Board

My grandfather was a quiet man, who spent his life working hard right up until the union decided they didn't like the image of an old man climbing a crane. He was diagnosed with cancer shortly after his retirement, though his body and mind had worn his labour of love would continue right through his burial. For every coin he left worth its weight in gold, the memory he would endow of a reserved fierceness and will undying, speaks to power something far more important. A weight carried for generations on the shoulders of men women and children, living and dying for no more than the feeling of soil passing through their fingers. To families left with a fortune in spirit, not the knowledge but living proof, that we are judged for who we are when nobody is watching. Centuries of work for nothing, for nothing.

Simple as it is piercing, we all know well that nothing lasts forever. The same rising and setting sun that weighs heavy on our spirit like bills on an overdrawn chequing account, be the heat on our skin and the light in our eyes. There is but one door through which we may pass, the door through which new things become old and dirty becomes clean. Sweet nothing, means more than nothing, and it means more than anything. Can something be everything and nothing all at once?

Once upon a time, it is said that fire was stolen from the gods themselves and brought down from the mountains to keep mankind warm. Why is it that humanity's oldest and most important stories, conceal their truths so tightly woven in whimsy? Fairy tales, invitations to curiosity so magnetic a man threatens to lose sight of their fatal irony that belies them. Knowledge of the past is knowledge of the future, power which can only be grasped as they meet in the middle, as two edges of Damocles' sword. Sharp as it is untouchable, as Christ among the lepers and lames.

When I was in grade school, I was very fortunate to have the opportunity to have art classes regularly. Though I never had much talent drawing, it was always enjoyable working with colour to try and recreate the images in my mind. Pencil crayons on white paper, were my weapons of choice for their precise tips, and relative accessibility. One particular drawing, as assigned in a religion class was of the virgin Mary. Finally revealed to a young mind exploring the world, was a purpose for the white pencil crayon. Though none of my classmates had elected to sharpen their edges and create a better contrast in the white background with this curious tool, after a few coats I found that the texture of my product very satisfying.

There may be no more fearsome task for a human being, than raising good children. Somewhere in the grey and murky waters between nature and nurture, the future is invented in the imagination of children. Prophecy subtly etched into the book of life, dreams set in motion like a toy car travelling down a track. Left to chase after screeching tires, burnout marks and the crying driver is the pit crew we call parents. Parenthood is to be the wind and rain to a delicate baby, just as to be a parent is to be at the mercy of the wind and the rain. A crazy game of love and war, with consequences stretching farther than the eye can see in all directions. Right and wrong fade away as the waters of life settle in the middle, where ends justify means and lessons are learned like scars. Beauty is impenetrable in motion, to all but who make time stand still.
