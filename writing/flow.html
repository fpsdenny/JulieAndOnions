Writing Essays Poems

The conclusion from Burry was, to be spectacular, you had to be spectacularly unusual.

Michael Lewis, The Big Short

Water will always flow along the path of least resistance. As will electrical energy, another force which drives our movement, our thoughts, and feelings. What a delicate instrument this body is, and what an honour and pleasure it is to observe through the lens of conscience where it will go and what it will do. This web of life that we are all caught in, a system so intricate yet powerful, with a capacity beyond our wildest imagination. A mystery so large and so captivating, that if you stopped paying attention it might fade away as if it never existed at all.

The willow submits to the wind and prospers until one day
it is many willows; a wall against the wind.
This is the willow’s purpose.

Frank Herbert, Dune

To bear a cross, carries meaning far beyond the lifetime of Jesus, if he lived at all. The story left over, told by a handful of loyal witnesses. All 6 of them, just men like you and me. Just people, trying to find a way in life, do the right thing. They had a conscience just like the rest of us, that little voice that maybe never makes a difference to what we do, or think, or say. But deep down, each of us knows that's who we are. What lives in the shadows, and carries us through this bizarre, tumultuous ride from birth to death. Is it destiny? We well never know

Everybody wanna be a bodybuilder

but don't nobody wanna lift no heavy ass weight

Ronnie Coleman

One of the men who had the greatest impact on all of human history. Did nothing but walk, and talk. His message of peace, of righteousness, humility, to love God and treat everyone as yourself. Do you think he knew what he did would alter political history? Change the human way of being for centuries, in such a profound way? The influence of Christ permeates far beyond the reaches of any church, even the one in his name. If we look at his conscience, as what could have only been a poor soul just like ours that lived a life, was true to himself, and saw himself die nailed to a wooden cross. And for what? A sacrifice, to show people the value of love. That try as they want, nobody can stop you from being your true self. Nobody can ever take your heart.

If you want to see the measure of a man,

look at how he treats his inferiors,

not his equals

J.K. Rowling
