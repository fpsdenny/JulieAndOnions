The Doghouse

2026

Writing Essays Poems

You know, the floor ain't going nowhere It's a great place to be if you just want to lay there Without rhyme or reason, for a thousand yard stare Because death doesn't want me and Taylor Swift doesn't care Just broken hearts and internal affairs

I just don't get why the sun always glares If I could just grow up, they told me I'll get there Begin a long walk up a million stairs But I just want to be lonely, and situationally aware Don't tell me my ghost, isn't playing fair I can't afford to keep him in my underground lair He wants a Ferrari, I want a Porsche So he threatens my rates like his last name's Warsh And the room gets cold like we aren't headed North

I bought a new houseplant, and it gave me the finger I think I might be crazy but it keeps getting bigger I still might go short if I see the right trigger I'd vibe code my dreams if they made any sense I'll sleep with absurdity and dig holes instead Because politics and pillowtalk made my bed There's always tomorrow, to hit my head Go flowers for Algernon, and forget what I've said Go sit at my desk, and read about the Fed Tell my Claude agent I made it too wet to wed

Nobody likes poetry, that's what they said So maybe I'll just chill here instead With my cowboys and ink, and work when I'm dead
