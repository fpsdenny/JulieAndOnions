ConvexityEngine

market infrastructure · under construction

The firm is currently building out a set of market analysis and research tools. We are seeking to provide a comprehensive, sophisticated dashboard to allow small teams to capitalize on macro-trends.

Attention to detail is key to anticipating when market discontinuities, which can be extremely profitable events for small firms and offer extensive protections in specific hedging scenarios. However, staying on top of markets and keeping an upright view of the global macro state can be difficult. Over the last few years, we have sought to translate the view that helped catch the trades that make the market.

The firm's thesis centers around operational flexibility, and the synthesis of orthodox economic ideas with the mathematical reason capacity of artificial intelligence to rise to the challenge of a rapidly changing digital domain. Make no mistake, we are not offering a conventional analytical approach to trading. We have formulated over the years a complete ideological map of street-level macro trends, to create an edge for smaller traders and teams to capitalize in a financial world dominated by quants, investment banks and high frequency trading algorithms.

AI alone will not be enough to consistently create profits over the next few years. If you have a personal assistant, so does the guy on the other end of your trade. It's always the economy, stupid. We give you the data, you make the trade.
