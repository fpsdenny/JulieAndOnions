Folded Vector Field Analysis (FVFA)

private research software · active

Folded Vector Field Analysis (FVFA) is a private software research project: an engine for deciding whether patterns claimed in complex natural systems are real. Currently under domain-free development.

So far, every claim brought before the engine has died at its bar. That is the design working: a system you intend to trust has to be hard to convince, and the honest "no" is the foundation the eventual "yes" will stand on.

FVFA is the exclusive property of Hayden Kerr. It is research software; nothing it produces is operational guidance of any kind.
