The Subaru

2005 Subaru Impreza RS 2.5 · rebuilt 2024

The work
  • Replaced front and rear stabilizer bars, sanded to bare metal and repainted
  • New stabilizer bar links to front control arms and rear lateral links
  • Replaced both front control arms and ball joints
  • Replaced both rear lateral links
  • Replaced all 4 struts, with pre-assembled modules
  • Replaced both front tie rods, inner and outer
  • Replaced both front steering knuckles
  • Replaced all 4 wheel bearings (semi-assembled, inserted with a framing hammer and bearing press kit)
  • Replaced all 4 CV axles
  • Topped off front and rear differentials with fluid
  • Installed 4 new wheel hubs
  • Installed new battery tie down
  • Sourced new 12.9 grade bolts, as many had to be cut off
  • Replaced rear right brake caliper
  • Replaced hardware for parking brake

This was a long and tedious process, which encompassed me learning many new skills, including but not limited to

  • FCAW welder
  • Pneumatic impact driver
  • Pneumatic air chisel
  • Cutoff tool
  • Specific use case wrenches, such as for tie rods
  • Torching, cutting or drilling out high-grade automotive bolts
