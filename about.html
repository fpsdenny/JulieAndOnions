The host

My name is Hayden Kerr.

I have spent decade in professional kitchens, from small towns to corporate as a sous chef. Though I never received a formal education, I have had the blessing of some excellent mentors, an abundance of challenges and above all else learning opportunities.

As I move forward in life, this website serves as a conscious reflection of the passion and artistry that flows through restaurants, under the intense duress that is comes as part of the lifestyle that shaped my step into adulthood. Those experiences will always serve me, and I treasure the stress response that was conditioned in my time spent leading hectic dinner services.

Beginning in late 2019, I opened my first brokerage account and began my foray into the world of finance, seeking to teach myself to be a stock operator in the most stripped down sense. This emerged from a long-standing interest in markets and geopolitics. I used to make my parents buy only Dole bananas when I was 6, because I would eat 3 bunches a week. They had told me they would invest in Dole at my advice, though I discovered much later this wasn't true, an interest was sparked. Later when I was 13, me and some friends on the bus decided we'd run a competition on a stock simulator app. I was all in on Nvidia before we forgot about it, as 13 year olds do.

Since putting my own money on the line, I have once again had the blessing of many lessons. Which is to say, it took me all this time to barely pull myself back into the green. A handful of big wins have supported my as my strategy was refined, among them shorting $SPY in early 2020, as I was sitting in my university gym between sets (not knowing it would be my last session for several years). Buying OTM puts in $SHY going into the February 2022 Fed meeting (rates were indeed raised, against a complete market consensus). Shorting $UAL in early 2025, and long $CL options in February 2026 (in at $66.5, out at $115.5).

Now that isn't saying a lot. But here I am consolidating what I have learned, and leaving the evidence here to speak for itself.

I have been around politics since I could walk, and my writing and worldview is largely an expression of the responsibility I carry from these early experiences. I am jaded, cynical, and conceited. In time and with some maturity, I have realized these traits flourish when embellished with offbeat philosophy and flagrant humor.

On a more personal note, I enjoy grimy rap music, dad rock, the occasional movie, and a good scotch. Most of my time outside work is spent on the ski hill or the golf course. I have a small collection of reptiles and houseplants I have been accumulating since high school

The project

This is a living graveyard. For love in every form that I've learned how to express. It's found its way onto the internet now — take your time, it's got nowhere to be.

This project is model for identity in the age of hyper-securitization. My own Wendy's twitter, the incorporation of my interest, character, hobbies and business.
