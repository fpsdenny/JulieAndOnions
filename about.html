The host

My name is Hayden Kerr.

I have spent a decade in professional kitchens, from small town café baker to big-city corporate sous-chef. Though I never received a formal education, I have had the blessing of some excellent mentors, an abundance of challenges and above all else learning opportunities. This experience has shaped my adult life, and though I do not have the ambitions to become a great chef, I spend lots of time cooking and experimenting with new ingredients and techniques.

There are a wide array of hobbies, passions and interests that I seek to share on this platform I have developed, and I am always looking for more. Julie and Onions was conceived in a kitchen, unsurprisingly while I was julienning onions, and I knew that was what my business would be called. It cemented itself when I was offered a discount at Home Depot for some tools, if I filled out a form with a business name. Though there is currently no products or services offered in the name, the scope of my ambitions for this entity runs deep.

I have dedicated much of my time outside my professional life to finance, and trading. Beginning in late 2019, I opened my first brokerage account and began my foray into the world of finance, seeking to teach myself to be a stock operator in the most stripped down sense. This emerged from a long-standing interest in markets and geopolitics. I used to make my parents buy only Dole bananas when I was 6, because I would eat 3 bunches a week. They had told me they would invest in Dole at my advice, though I discovered much later this wasn't true, an interest was sparked. Later when I was 13, me and some friends on the bus decided we'd run a competition on a stock simulator app. I was all in on Nvidia before we forgot about it, as 13 year olds do.

Since putting my own money on the line, I have once again had the blessing of many lessons. Which is to say, it took me all this time to barely pull myself back into the green. A handful of big wins have supported my as my strategy was refined, among them shorting $SPY in early 2020, as I was sitting in my university gym between sets (not knowing it would be my last session for several years). Buying OTM puts in $SHY going into the February 2022 Fed meeting (rates were indeed raised, against a complete market consensus). Shorting $UAL in early 2025, and long $CL options in February 2026 (in at $66.5, out at $115.5).

Now that isn't saying a lot. But here I am consolidating what I have learned, and leaving the evidence of what I am doing here to speak for itself. I manage proprietary capital and quite enjoy the discretion in my strategy that this offers.

I have been around politics since I could walk, and my writing and worldview is largely an expression of the responsibility I carry from these early experiences. I am jaded, cynical, and conceited. In time and with some maturity, I have realized these traits flourish when embellished with offbeat philosophy and flagrant humor.

On a more personal note, I enjoy grimy rap music, dad rock, the occasional movie, and a good scotch. Most of my time outside work is spent on the ski hill or the golf course. I have a small collection of reptiles and houseplants that keep me company at my computer.

The project

This website is model for identity in the age of hyper-securitization. A place outside social media, off the beaten path where my interests, hobbies, character and business exist together. Here I am exposed, to me it is a living graveyard for love in every form I've learned how to express

This market serves as my conscious reflection on the passion and artistry that flows through restaurants. Under heavy duress, constant and varied demands and responsibilities, and while always watching the clock cooks around the world deliver food that shapes some of our fondest memories.

While my interests and passions never could sit still in the kitchen, nevertheless I carry those experiences and stress response in everything I do. So I gave them a place to live that felt like a restaurant.
