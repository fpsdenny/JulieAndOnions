The host

My name is Hayden Kerr.

Most people know me as a cook, but this isn't a kitchen, and I'm not paid to be here.

I am Canadian, and I love emo music, literature, being cold and miserable, talking shit and slicing onions.

This website is an exploration of two of my wild passions, trading/finance and the American Deep South. I spend my days cooking professionally, watching price charts, vibe-coding analysis tools and setting up trades. For reasons that cannot be ascertained, I spend my nights dreaming of bubbly blonde women with thick homely accents, cajun shrimp gumbo, warm biscuits, and the unbridled, violent urge towards self-determination.

I was halfway done a biology degree when I decided my time was better spent being a burnout stoner. That made a perfect fit at the time to climb the ladder at my job as a corporate line cook on the closing shift. The shotgun wedding that ensued made up some of the best years of my life.

When I was a kid, I used to make my parents buy only Dole bananas (of which I ate 3 bunches a week) because I was convinced I could make the stock go up. So naturally, it would happen that 15 years later, sitting on a bus to Lake Louise nursing a wicked hangover, I would decide that was a good time to open a brokerage account.

Fast-forward to my first options trade a couple months later, I got the bright idea from reddit to bet that a virus surfacing in Chinese news would drop the S&P 500 by 100 points. My last winning trade was crude oil in early 2026, because I shoot like dragonflies hunt. Don't count on me writing about markets often, because better analysis with better data do a better job. Politics and philosophy is more of my wheelhouse anyway, since I am bitter, jaded, and cynical. Honest perspective, seasoned with offbeat wisdom and embellished with flagrant humour is the name of the game here.
